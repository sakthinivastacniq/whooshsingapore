About Whoosh Singapore

Reliable work starts with a team that sees the whole site.

Founded in 2008 by S. Thiruchelvam, Whoosh Singapore has grown into a building services contractor supporting residential, commercial and industrial properties. Our team brings multiple services together with clear coordination, practical planning and care for the people using each space.

From high-rise residential painting to electrical installation and reinstatement, we work to help clients maintain, improve and hand over properties with confidence.
