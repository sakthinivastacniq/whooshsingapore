ABOUT WHOOSH

Building trust.
Delivering excellence.

Whoosh Singapore Pte Ltd provides integrated building, engineering and facility solutions for residential, commercial and industrial properties. Founded in 2008 by S. Thiruchelvam, we have grown into a team of more than 85 skilled professionals.

From repair and redecoration to M&E services and reinstatement, we bring clear coordination and practical site planning to every job.
